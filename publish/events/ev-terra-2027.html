01活动介绍

第14届世界土建筑大会定于 2027 年 4 月 19 日至 22 日在阿联酋艾因举行。原定 2026 年，因区域空域波动与国际参与组织方面的运行和后勤原因延期至 2027 年 4 月。大会主题为“Managing Change in Earthen Cultural Landscapes”，设置土建筑遗产与技艺传承、土建筑文化景观保护、土建筑创新等议题，并配套海报、数字材料、边会和实地活动。

02核验依据

The 14th World Congress on Earthen Architecture, Terra Congress, will take place from 19-22 April 2027 in Al Ain, United Arab Emirates (UAE). Initially planned for 2026, it was postponed to April 2027 due to operational and logistical reasons linked to regional airspace volatility and international participation. Organised by ICOMOS ISCEAH (International Scientific Committee on Earthen Architectural Heritage) and the Department of Culture and Tourism-Abu Dhabi, it explores the theme "Managing Change in Earthen Cultural Landscapes".

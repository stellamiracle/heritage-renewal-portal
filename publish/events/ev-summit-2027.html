01活动介绍

由 Europa Nostra 主办、波兰国际文化中心（ICC）作为本地主办方承办的欧洲文化遗产年度峰会，2027 年 6 月 6 日至 9 日在波兰克拉科夫举行，并获欧盟与波兰文化和国家遗产部支持。峰会聚焦欧洲文化遗产保护与利用的政策合作与专业交流。

02核验依据

The European Cultural Heritage Summit 2027 will be held from 6 to 9 June in Krakow, Poland. Organised by Europa Nostra in collaboration with the International Cultural Centre (ICC) as the Polish host, the Summit is supported by the European Union and the Ministry of Culture and National Heritage of Poland.

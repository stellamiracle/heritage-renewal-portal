01活动介绍

2027 年 3 月 17 日至 21 日在罗马举行《罗马条约》签署 70 周年纪念活动。其中 3 月 18 日至 19 日为欧盟国家元首和政府首脑峰会，3 月 20 日举行“欧洲大游行”。Europa Nostra 作为联合文化遗产机构参与该系列纪念活动。

02核验依据

The celebration of the 70th anniversary of the Treaties of Rome will take place from 17 to 21 March 2027, with the European Summit of the EU Heads of State and Government taking place on 18-19 March, and a March for Europe taking place on 20 March. Europa Nostra will contribute to this Jubilee with a series of events.

01活动介绍

CONCRETO 项目为期三年合作的结业活动于 2026 年 12 月 4 日在布鲁塞尔举行，面向混凝土遗产保护领域的从业者、教育者、学生与合作伙伴，内容包括主题讨论、经验交流、人脉拓展与技术交流，并同步开放活动报名。

02核验依据

CONCRETO Academy Final Event. Join us on December 4th in Brussels for the CONCRETO Academy Final Event! As we bring three years of collaboration, learning, and knowledge exchange to a close, we invite you to celebrate the achievements of the CONCRETO project and look ahead to the future of concrete heritage conservation.
